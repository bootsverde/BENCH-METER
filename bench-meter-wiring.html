Ladder scaling (volts at each input)

ACV = (V_CH1 − 1.000) × 125 → 0–500 V OL if ACV > 520 DCV = V_CH2 × 3.000 (trim if needed) OL if V_CH2 > 9.98 Ω = 2000 × V_CH3 / (V_CH4 − V_CH3) OL if V_CH3 > 0.99 × V_CH4 BEEP (Y1) = Ω < 30

Inputs are 13-bit (1.22 mV/count). Expect about 0.15 V/count and ±1% on AC (the VACT's rating), about 4 mV/count on DC, and the best ohms accuracy from 10 Ω to 50 kΩ. Drive OUT CH1 at full scale (10 V).

Before you power it up

  • Only the AC jacks, F1/F2 and the VACT input are rated for 480 V. Use 600 V wire there and keep it physically apart from the low-voltage wiring.
  • The DC and Ω COM jacks are tied to the 24 V 0 V rail. If you earth that rail, those black jacks are at earth, the same as a bench scope's ground clip.
  • Test the AC channel first on 120 V against a known-good meter before you go anywhere near 480 V.
  • Keep a CAT III/IV handheld for live troubleshooting. This meter is for the bench.
