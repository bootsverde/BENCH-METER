Wiring behind the panel

  • S1 POWER switches the 120 VAC line (L) between the fuse and the PSB24. The GCX3300 contact block is rated for mains, but check that it's 1 N.O. and order a second block if you want to switch both L and N.
  • PL1 ON goes across +24 V / 0 V (TB10 / TB12 on the stripboard). It lights when the 24 V supply is up.
  • PB1, PB2 each go from +24 V (stripboard TB11 "PB") to CPU X1 / X2.
  • BZ1: (+) to CPU Y1, (−) to 0 V (stripboard TB13 "BUZ−").
  • J1–J4 go to the stripboard through F3 / F4 as before. J5 / J6 go only to F1 / F2 and the VACT.

Enclosure

  • Front: any metal enclosure with a 16 × 12 in (406 × 305 mm) or larger face. Bond the door to the enclosure ground with a braided strap.
  • Depth: at least 6 in (150 mm). The EA7-T8C needs 44 mm behind the panel, plus room for the EA-2CBL connector. The DIN rail parts (CLICK, PSB24, VACT, fuse holders) mount on the back plate.
  • Cord: bring the 120 VAC cord in through a strain-relief cord grip on a side wall, with the 2 A fuse holder next to it, not on the front.
  • Panel thickness: check the maximum for the HMI mounting clips and the Pomona jacks before choosing plate thicker than 14 ga (1.9 mm).

Before cutting

  1. The drawing is proportional but not full size, since the panel is bigger than a sheet of paper. Lay out the holes from the hole schedule with a square and centre punch, then hold each part up to its mark before drilling.
  2. Check the EA7-T8C cutout against the panel's own instructions or datasheet. The figures here are from AutomationDirect's EA7-T8C spec sheet.
  3. Behind the panel, keep the 22 mm device bodies (about 60 mm deep) clear of the HMI's rear housing, which is 232.6 × 177.6 mm, centred on the cutout.
