Stripboard layout · BusBoard ST2 (Digi-Key 4526-ST2-ND) · 100 × 80 mm · 31 strips × 39 holes

Bench Meter Stripboard

All the low-voltage conditioning (DC volts, ohms, the VACT loop resistor) and 24 V distribution on one ST2 stripboard, from the input jacks' fuses to the C0-4AD2DA-2 terminals. Positions are given as strip / hole: strips 1–31 run top to bottom, holes 1–39 left to right, viewed from the component side.

120 VAC stays off this board

Stripboard strips are only about 1 mm apart, the holes aren't plated, and you can't route an isolation slot. That's fine for 24 V, but not for mains. Run the cord through a panel-mount 5×20 fuse holder (0218002.HXP, 2 A slow-blow) and the panel switch straight to the PSB24's L / N / ⏚ terminals. The board starts at the PSB24's 24 V output.

Before power-up

  1. Hold the board up to a light and check every cut and scraped area. No copper bridges, and no solder bridges between strips.
  2. Ohmmeter checks with nothing connected: strip 2 (holes 1–5) to anything should read open. Strip 20 (+24) to any 0 V strip should read open. Strip 4 to strip 30 hole 25 should read 0 Ω (bus OK).
  3. Single-sided pads lift easily. Support the terminal blocks with a dab of epoxy or hot glue, and hold the block while you tighten screws.
  4. Apply 24 V only. Check +24 at TB10 and 0 V at TB12, then follow the same bring-up as the wiring page: DC channel, then ohms (1 kΩ gives about 3.33 V at CH3), then the VACT loop.
